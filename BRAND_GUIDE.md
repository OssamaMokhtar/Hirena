# Hirena Brand Guide

## Brand Identity

**Hirena** — AI-powered skills assessment & career pathing.

**Tagline:** Assess your skills. Find your path.

**Mission:** Empower every person to make evidence-based career decisions through AI-powered skills assessment and personalized career pathing. Bilingual. Accessible. MENA-first.

**Vision:** A world where career decisions are driven by data, not guesswork — where anyone can assess their skills against any opportunity and chart a clear path forward.

## Brand Voice

- **Clear, evidence-based, empowering.** Hirena doesn't guess. It measures. It shows you the gap. It charts the path. It's honest about limitations.
- **Bilingual confidence.** Arabic and English with equal weight. No translated-afterthought feel.
- **Professional but accessible.** Not academic. Not salesy. Practical, actionable, trustworthy.
- **MENA-rooted, global-ready.** Regional expertise and cultural awareness as strength, not limitation.

## Typography

- **Primary:** Inter (sans-serif) — clean, modern, highly legible in both Latin and Arabic scripts
- **Arabic:** Tajawal or IBM Plex Sans Arabic — Arabic-compatible, professional

## Logo Concept

**Text-based wordmark: "Hirena"** with a subtle icon suggestion:
- A ladder/scale motif (representing skills scale, climbing, progression)
- Or an upward arrow/curve (representing career path, growth, trajectory)
- Or an abstract "H" integrated with a scale/ladder element

Current: Text-based ("Hirena") is sufficient for MVP. Icon can be refined later.

## MENA Positioning

- Arabic and English with equal weight — not English-first with Arabic translation
- RTL layout done right (CSS logical properties, not flipped LTR)
- Cultural context in UX (prayer times, weekend Fri-Sat, Ramadan considerations for product usage patterns)
- GCC market as entry market, with regional expertise as moat

## Messaging Framework

### Primary Message
"Assess your skills against any job. See your gap. Find your path."

### Supporting Messages
- "Career decisions built on evidence, not guesswork."
- "Paste any job description. See exactly where you stand."
- "Your skills, measured. Your gap, clear. Your path, mapped."
- "Bilingual. MENA-first. Built for the way people work in the Gulf."

### Positioning Statement
"For professionals in the MENA region who want to make evidence-based career decisions, Hirena is an AI-powered skills assessment and career pathing platform that measures your skills against any job description, shows your gaps clearly, and maps your path forward — because career decisions should be driven by data, not guesses."

### Competitive Positioning (1-liner)
"Hirena is to skills assessment what Kickresume is to resumes — but for skills, with career pathing, bilingual MENA-first, and a clear gap analysis you can act on."
