# Privacy Policy

**Status:** Draft — for prototype phase. Not a substitute for legal advice.

## Data We Collect

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| CV/resume data | Career assessment, skill extraction | Per user; deletable on request |
| Cognitive assessment results | Career score calculation | Per user; deletable on request |
| Skill profile data | Career profiling, radar chart | Per user; deletable on request |
| Market insights queries | Career guidance | Aggregated, anonymized |
| LLM prompts/responses | CV parsing, market insights (server-side only) | Minimized; not persisted by default |

## Data Storage

- CV and assessment data is stored in the application's database (prototype: local development only).
- No data is shared with third parties except as required for LLM inference (Gemini API, server-side only).
- CV data is PII under UAE PDPL and handled accordingly.

## Your Rights

Under UAE PDPL and applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data (subject to retention requirements)
- Withdraw consent (where processing is consent-based)

## Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the maintainer.
