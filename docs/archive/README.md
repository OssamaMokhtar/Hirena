# Archive — historical (before 23 Sep 2026)

These documents describe Hirena before the text-only decision (ADR-001 in [../10-decision-log.md](../10-decision-log.md)). They include video interviews, voice and facial analysis, and multimodal fusion, all of which were **removed** because inferring emotions in education or employment contexts is prohibited by EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f).

They are kept to show how the design evolved. They do **not** describe the current product; see [../README.md](../README.md).
