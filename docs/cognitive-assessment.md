# Cognitive Assessment — CareerOracle

**Status:** Documentation — validation pending.

## What It Measures

CareerOracle's cognitive assessment establishes a baseline for:

- **Logical reasoning** — the ability to reason systematically, identify patterns, draw conclusions from evidence
- **Verbal reasoning** — the ability to understand and reason with language, comprehend written information, evaluate arguments

These are used as part of the career score, alongside CV-derived experience and market demand data.

## Assessment Approach

The assessment is delivered as a series of questions/tasks that evaluate logical and verbal reasoning. Results are combined into a cognitive baseline that feeds the career score.

## Limitations

- This assessment is **not a validated psychometric instrument**. It is a prototype assessment designed for career guidance, not clinical or employment screening.
- Results should be interpreted as **one signal among several** — alongside CV-derived experience and market demand data.
- The assessment has **not been validated** against established measures of logical and verbal reasoning.
- Assessment results are **not deterministic** — they can be affected by fatigue, stress, language proficiency, and other factors.
